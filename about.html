MoonTech Life started with one belief: that tech education shouldn't just be about content; it should be about belonging, support, and real outcomes. We build more than courses. We build futures.

OUR MISSION

Expand access to tech education

To empower the next generation of African tech professionals through free, high quality, community driven education that leads to real career outcomes not just certificates.

OUR VISION

A more inclusive tech future

A world where geography, income, or background never limits your access to world-class tech education. We are building a community that makes that possible, one person at a time.

What We Do

We create learning and community experiences that help you grow in tech and take your next step with confidence.

Learning Programs

Practical online programs and challenges designed for real-world skills.

Community Support

A safe and active community to ask questions, share wins, and grow together.

Opportunities

Access to internships, projects, and industry connections.

Continuous Growth

Ongoing resources, feedback, and events to help you keep building.

What We Stand For

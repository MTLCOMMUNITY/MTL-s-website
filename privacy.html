Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. This policy outlines how MoonTechLife collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you interact with our website, courses, and community programs.

1. Introduction

Welcome to MoonTechLife. We are a collaborative technology community dedicated to helping learners and transitioning professionals build practical, real-world skills in tech.

We value your trust and are committed to handling your personal information responsibly and transparently. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you visit our website, register for our courses, join our challenges, or communicate with our community.

2. Information We May Collect

We only collect personal information that is reasonably necessary to provide you with our educational programs, community activities, and support services. This may include:

  • Contact Information: Your full name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, and country/city of residence.
  • Registration Data: Details submitted through course registration forms, program waitlists (such as the 100 Days Tech Challenge), and onboarding questionnaires.
  • Interactive Submissions: Responses provided when using our interactive tools, such as the Course Recommendation Quiz or community feedback surveys.
  • Communications: Inquiries, feedback, support messages, or emails you send directly to our team.
  • Technical & Usage Information: Basic technical logs automatically collected when accessing the website, such as device type, browser version, operating system, pages visited, and general access timestamps to ensure website security and performance.

3. How Information Is Used

We use the information we collect for legitimate community and educational purposes, including:

  • Processing course and program registrations, challenge cohort onboarding, and waitlist notifications.
  • Responding to your inquiries, support requests, and community questions.
  • Sending essential announcements, schedule updates, curriculum materials, and mentorship details.
  • Operating, evaluating, and improving our website, educational tools, and learning experiences.
  • Monitoring and safeguarding the technical integrity and security of our platform against malicious activities.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website uses standard browser storage and essential cookies to ensure key site features work reliably (such as remembering modal states, filter selections, and navigation preferences). We do not use third-party invasive advertising trackers. You can adjust your browser settings to decline cookies if you prefer, though some parts of the site may function with reduced convenience.

5. Third-Party Services

To deliver a reliable online experience, we may rely on trusted third-party service providers for specific operational functions, such as website hosting, content delivery networks (CDNs), form processing, and community communication channels (e.g., Telegram and official social media platforms). These providers only process information as necessary to perform their respective services for MoonTechLife.

6. Data Sharing & Disclosure

MoonTechLife does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers or advertisers. We only share personal data:

  • With vetted service providers who assist in operating our community platforms, under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, regulation, subpoena, or legal process.
  • To protect the safety, rights, and security of MoonTechLife, our learners, and the general public.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate administrative, technical, and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please note that no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely infallible, and we encourage you to protect your login details and personal accounts.

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably needed to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, support your participation in community programs, maintain academic or completion records, and comply with any applicable legal or operational obligations.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access to the data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate information, or request the deletion of your personal data from our active records. To exercise any of these rights, please reach out to us using the contact details provided below.

10. Children's Privacy

MoonTechLife programs and website are intended for students, young adults, and professionals seeking career growth. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without verified parental or guardian consent. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective action.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our community offerings, technology, or legal requirements. When updates occur, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
